About Thandolwethu Njabulo Mdaki

I am Thandolwethu Njabulo Mdaki, a third-year BSc Agriculture student specialising in Animal Sciences at the University of Venda. My interest in agriculture developed through early exposure to livestock and crop production, and it has grown into a scientific interest in animal nutrition, production, health, reproduction, breeding and genetics.

I am particularly interested in reproductive biotechnology, including semen evaluation and preservation, artificial insemination, fertilisation and embryo technologies. Alongside this developing focus, I continue to build a broad understanding of livestock production systems and the biological, environmental and economic factors that influence animal performance.

Through academic projects, supervised practical activities, field exposure and industry engagement, I am developing the knowledge and professional discipline needed to contribute meaningfully to Animal Science. I value animal welfare, scientific accuracy, continuous learning and solutions that can work under real production conditions.

I have also developed strong computer and digital-working skills through university activities. Preparing assignments, research reports, presentations and practical documents has strengthened my ability to use word-processing, presentation, spreadsheet, online-research and digital communication tools accurately and efficiently.
